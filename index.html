Tool Notes — Read Before You Use Them

Backlink Validator and DR Checker use built-in local lists. They are not live APIs and they do not crawl the internet.

Bulk DR Checker has no built-in list. Upload your own dataset first; missing domains get the placeholder default DR 70, which is not a measured DR.

DR Formatter does not check DR, it formats lists. Worker ↔ Buyer Matcher does not distribute links, it matches sheets. URL → Domain Extractor extracts domains from URLs (its old title says the reverse) and a bare domain gives blank output.

SEO Link Comparator does not fetch live links; it compares the two lists you give it.

All tools run in your browser. Nothing you paste or upload is sent to a LinkXcel server, because there is no server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the DR results live?

No. The DR Checker uses a built-in local list, and the Bulk DR Checker searches a dataset you upload yourself. Neither calls a live DR API, so a domain that is not in the list is not a measured result — in Bulk DR it gets the placeholder default DR 70.

What dataset does the Bulk DR Checker need?

Your own CSV or Excel file of domains and DR values. Upload it once with Load Dataset; it is saved in your browser (localStorage). After that, paste domains in bulk to search it, export results, or clear the stored dataset.

Why is the “DR” post actually a formatter?

That is one of three title mismatches on the original posts, and LinkXcel says so plainly. The DR Formatter does not check DR — it formats lines like “https://example.com 45” into “example.com”: 45, so you can build a DR database.

Why is the “Link Distribution Tool” actually a matcher?

Second title mismatch. It does not distribute links. It matches a Worker Master Sheet against a Buyer Demo Sheet (exact URL first, then domain fallback) and exports one sheet per worker, plus Summary and Not_Found.

Why does the “Domain To URL Converter” extract domains?

Third title mismatch. It does the reverse of its title: it extracts domains FROM full URLs. Paste full URLs — a bare domain with no scheme gives blank output.

Does the SEO Link Comparator fetch live links?

No. Despite “Live” in its name, it only compares the two lists you paste or drop. It never fetches a page from the internet.

How do profile backlink teams use LinkXcel?

A team lead collects worker links, validates them against the buyer list, filters by DR, matches worker sheets to buyer sheets, shuffles lists for distribution across the team, removes duplicates, and exports the finished sheets for the buyer — all free, in the browser, with no sign-up.
