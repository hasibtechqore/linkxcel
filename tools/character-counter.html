Character Counter

Type or paste text and see live counts of characters, words, sentences, paragraphs, and lines.

Runs entirely in your browser — your text never leaves your device. Counting method: words are whitespace-separated tokens; sentences are text blocks ending in . ! or ? (a final block without a terminator still counts as one sentence); paragraphs are blocks separated by blank lines; lines are newline-separated lines.
