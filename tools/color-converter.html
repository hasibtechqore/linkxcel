Color Converter

Convert a color between HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK — change any format and convert from it.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Values are clamped to their valid ranges (RGB 0–255, HSL hue 0–360, saturation/lightness 0–100%, CMYK 0–100%). Conversions are mathematical equivalents of the same color, not a different shade.
