Image Compressor

Shrink JPG, PNG and WebP images right here in your browser — pick a quality, optionally resize, and download the smaller file.

100% private: your images never leave this device. Compression runs on a canvas in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded to any server. Honest limit: re-encoding a PNG as PNG is lossless, so it shrinks little; choosing JPEG or WebP output saves much more. Very large files (over about 30 MB) may strain the browser tab, so prefer smaller images for smooth compression.
