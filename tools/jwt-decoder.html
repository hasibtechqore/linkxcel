JWT Decoder

Read a JWT's header and payload claims without sending the token anywhere.

Important — read before pasting: this tool does NOT verify the signature, so a decoded token is not proof that it is genuine or unmodified. Decoding happens offline, entirely in your browser, and nothing is uploaded — but never paste a token that contains secrets or gives access to a production account. For real tokens, prefer your own trusted debugger.

The header and payload are only Base64URL-decoded JSON. The exp claim (if present) is shown as a human date in both UTC and your browser's local time, with an expired / not-expired status.
