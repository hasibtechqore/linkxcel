Regex Tester

Test a regular expression against sample text and see every match, its position, and highlighted results.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Input is capped (pattern 1,000 characters, test text 50,000 characters) and matches are capped at 1,000 to keep the page responsive. Highlighted output is built with safe DOM nodes, never by injecting your text as HTML.
