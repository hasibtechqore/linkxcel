Text Case Converter

Convert text between 8 cases: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case, and Capitalized Words.

Runs entirely in your browser — your text never leaves your device. Title Case keeps small words (a, an, the, and, or, of, to, in, for, on, at, by) lowercase except at the start or end; Capitalized Words capitalizes every word. Apostrophes are handled so "don't" becomes "Don't", not "Don'T".
