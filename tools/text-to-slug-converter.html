Text to Slug Converter

Turn any heading or title into a clean, URL-friendly slug for SEO-friendly links.

Runs entirely in your browser — your text never leaves your device. Slug rules: lowercase, diacritics stripped (café → cafe), spaces and underscores become hyphens, other non-alphanumeric characters removed, and no leading, trailing, or double hyphens. This creates a slug only — it does not check whether the URL already exists.
