URL Encoder / Decoder

Make text safe for URLs, or turn a percent-encoded URL back into readable text.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. encodeURIComponent encodes everything except A–Z a–z 0–9 - _ . ! ~ * ' ( ) — use it for a single query value. encodeURI keeps a full URL's structure (: / ? & = #) intact and only encodes the unsafe parts. Decoding a malformed sequence (like a lone %) shows an error instead of crashing.
