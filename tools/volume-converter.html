Volume Converter

Convert volume units — liters, US gallons, milliliters, pints, cups, fluid ounces and cubic meters — results update as you type.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Gallons, pints, cups and fluid ounces here are US customary measures, not imperial ones, so a US gallon is 3.785 liters, not 4.546.
