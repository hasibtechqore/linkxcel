Word Counter

Paste text and see word, character, sentence, and paragraph counts plus an estimated reading time.

Runs entirely in your browser — your text never leaves your device. Reading time assumes 200 words per minute (a common average reading speed); actual reading time varies by reader and text difficulty. It is an estimate, not a measurement.
